Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Discover Dine In

  • From her early days cooking alongside her grandmother to becoming the World Dessert Champion of Australia, Chef Brooke Silk's culinary journey is as rich and flavorful as her dishes.

    With 18 years under her apron, including a distinguished tenure as a pastry chef, Brooke's passion evolved into Dine In.

    Dine In, born from Brooke's resilience during the first COVID lockdown, isn't just about meals; it's about crafting unforgettable dining moments.

    Discover the magic Brooke brings to every table, making every occasion not just a meal, but a memory.

  • Chef Brooke Silk transforms your home or venue into an exclusive dining area, crafting a unique menu from local, seasonal produce tailored just for you and your guests. Check out our ’Experiences’ page for all we have offer.

    Discover the seamless process that brings gourmet dining to your venue. From initial contact to post-event cleanup, learn how Chef Brooke Silk tailors every aspect of your event for a truly bespoke experience.

  • We service the Illawarra, South Coast, Highlands, and Greater Sydney.

    Dine In brings gourmet experiences to your doorstep, ensuring an unforgettable event wherever you are.

Planning Your Event

  • On your special day, expect seamless culinary service from start to finish.

    Chef Brooke and her team will arrive beforehand to set up, prepare the menu as planned, and serve your guests, ensuring a memorable dining experience.

    Our team will also handle the cleanup, leaving your space as we found it.

  • The cost varies depending on the menu selected, the number of guests, and specific service requirements.

    We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

    Contact us for a personalised quote tailored to your event's needs.

  • Your Dine In experience is confirmed with a 10% non-refundable booking fee of the total service cost.

    This secures your chosen date and Chef Brooke Silk’s exclusive services.

    Full payment is required 5 days prior to the event, along with your menu selections and dietary restrictions.

  • A standard kitchen setup is usually sufficient. We'll discuss specific requirements based on your chosen menu during the planning process. If additional equipment is needed, we can arrange rentals.

    For larger gatherings, we're here to assist in organizing rental tableware to accommodate everyone.

    Should you wish to use special tableware to add a personal touch to your event, we're more than happy to incorporate it, ensuring your occasion is exactly as you envision.

  • No, you don’t have to worry about the cleanup. Dine In ensures that your kitchen and dining area are left spotless, allowing you to relax and enjoy your event to the fullest.

  • Elevate your dining experience with professional waitress service. Essential for larger gatherings, this service enhances the flow, timing, and overall guest experience.

    For events with more than six guests, waitress service is mandatory to ensure seamless coordination and premium service standards.

    Waitress service includes:

    • Professional front-of-house guest service
    • Course coordination and food service
    • Clearing and resetting between courses
    • Refilling water and maintaining table presentation
    • Washing and packing away dining plates and cutlery used during meal service
    • Cleaning down kitchen benches and service areas
    • Sweeping and tidying kitchen floors post-service
    • Assisting with event setup and pack down
    • Supporting equipment loading and unloading
    • Working closely with Chef Brooke to ensure smooth timing and execution

    Waitress service is $135 for the first 2 hours, with $25 for each additional hour.

    Please note: Standard waitress service covers dining-related service and general event support. Full glassware washing and polishing or extensive bar service can be arranged as an additional service upon request.
