Expanding search bar

Click the magnifier. The circle stretches into a search field, the text fades in behind it, and a close mark appears. Press Escape or click away on an empty field to fold it back.

Behaviour

Click icon
Opens and focuses the field. With text entered, it submits.
Enter
Submits the search.
Escape
Closes and returns focus to the icon.
× button
Clears the text and closes.
Click away
Closes only if the field is empty.
