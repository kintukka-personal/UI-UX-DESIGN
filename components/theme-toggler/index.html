Try it

Click either button. The new theme grows out of the button you clicked as a circle until it covers the page.

The centre one uses data-duration="700" for a slower reveal.

Remembered

Your choice is saved in localStorage. Reload the page and it comes back without a flash.

With nothing saved, the page follows your system setting.

Fallback

Browsers without the View Transitions API, and visitors who prefer reduced motion, get an instant switch.
